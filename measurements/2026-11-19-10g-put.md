Two defects in `benchmarks/run_warp_benchmark.py` caused the failure:
1. **Whole-phase join timeout misapplied (`PHASE_JOIN_TIMEOUT_SEC = 120.0`):** `run_fixed_volume_phase()` called `join_all(threads, phase_label)` immediately from `t_start`, bounding the entire 10 GiB upload (160 × 64 MiB synced writes) to 120s instead of detecting an actual stall. At ~85 MiB/s across 8 workers, 10 GiB needs ~115–125s, so `join_all()` aborted at `120.04–120.05s` (`07:47:51.641Z -> 07:49:51.690Z`, `08:13:00.662Z -> 08:15:00.704Z`, `08:25:55.150Z -> 08:27:55.188Z`). Because `run_fixed_volume_phase()` spawned daemon threads with no `stop_event`, unjoined PUT workers continued running concurrently during `[10/10] get_10g`.
2. **30s per-request socket timeout with swallowed exceptions:** `run_fixed_volume_phase()` and `run_large_multipart_object_phase()` opened `http.client.HTTPConnection(..., timeout=30.0)` and ignored exceptions (`except Exception:`). On Windows, `FlushFileBuffers` + same-key stream relocation tail latency occasionally exceeded 30s (`max 28.62s` among completed parts in `61692.log`, plus silent >30s drops).
