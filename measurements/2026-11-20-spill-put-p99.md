# Spill index put latency across 1, 4, 16, and 64 writers (#327, step 1 of #316)

Run [44681098785](https://github.com/iskandarnorazli/Awanfs/actions/runs/44681098785), GitHub-hosted runners (`ubuntu-latest` and `windows-latest`, 4-vCPU VMs), comparing `redb` (`redb_spill::RedbSpill`) and `prod` (`s3_server::spill::SpillIndex` with the server's 64 MiB spill budget and 16 MiB memtable).

At 4 writers, `prod` put P99 drops 26x on Linux (674 us to 25.870 us, vs redb's 92.620 us) and 51x on Windows (1,123 us to 22.000 us, vs redb's 166 us) while `flushes` stays 1 across all runs, showing that thread oversubscription across 16 and 64 writers on 4-vCPU runners drives most of the 674 us and 1,123 us P99 rather than the single flush (though 4 writers remains above 1 writer's 3.790 us on Linux and 0.900 us on Windows).
