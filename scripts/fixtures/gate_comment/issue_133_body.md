The 10 GiB workload is 160 streamed 64 MiB parts at concurrency 64. Holding one 64 MiB part per in-flight request is about 4 GB at that concurrency, so buffered request bodies are the first thing to look at, not the payload cache: the cache takes nothing over 4 MiB.
